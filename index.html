MLR Portfolio Sync

MLR Portfolio Sync is a private, personal-use utility that synchronizes files authorized by the owner between Google Drive and a private automated workflow.

What the app does

The app uses Google Drive access to read and update the owner's approved project files during automated data-processing runs. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide public user accounts or sign-up.

Google user data

Google Drive data is accessed only for the synchronization and processing requested by the owner. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for independent purposes.

For details, see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
